## 2. Diagnosis

### 2.1 Two parts of `brain-x-loop` undid each other's label from 23:40 to 02:16

**What happened.** The 10-25 projects were split into tasks linked `after` their first task. From 23:40 to 02:16 UTC each tick
did this to 32 of them (`xl-lex-pds`, from the record):

```
23:49:11  drain/xl-lex-pds/before-after-25987/label   +waiting-issue  -needs-worker   (the route "Before after")
23:56:28  watchdog/xl-lex-pds/26100                   +needs-worker   -waiting-issue  "wait ended: no wait was written"
23:58:59  drain/xl-lex-pds/before-after-26109/label   +waiting-issue  -needs-worker
00:02:29  watchdog/xl-lex-pds/26152                   +needs-worker   -waiting-issue
...       12 labels of the route and 11 of the watchdog on this issue by 02:04
```

Each round is four events (two labels, two comments). In 24 h: 652 events of the route and 628 of the watchdog, 576 and
620 of them from 23:40 (76 of the route's are the first labelling of the afternoon). The loop's
`issue.get` calls were 4,724 in the 2.4 h from 23:40 against 8,719 in the 21.6 h before (1,936 an hour against 404),
on a database that runs one query at a time (`logs.query`, core lines). No issue was worked by it: each waited on an
issue at `needs-owner` or on one linked after such an issue.

**Why the system allowed it.** The module's reference has two rules that disagree. The watchdog's table: "`waiting-*` or
`needs-token`, and no live wait in `waits`" gets `needs-worker` back (`brain-loop.js`, "The watchdog"). The route "Before
after" puts `waiting-issue` and writes no wait, because its `after` links are the wait and "After done" ends it
("The routes"). The watchdog repairs only with the machine stopped, or when the label is not the issue's newest event
(`loopWatch`). The route first labelled them at 14:32 and the first watchdog write is 23:40:33; why not earlier is
not read (the machine's state between is not in the record), and the reading above is the likely one. Each rule has its tests; no test ran both on one issue.

**Would it have been caught without this run?** `measure.py` counted the four half-hours from 00:06 as "idle with work",
which would have read as a loop that did not start the machine. Nothing else counts writes that undo writes.
