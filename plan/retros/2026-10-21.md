**Why the system allowed it.** Three texts disagree:

`brain-x-loop` knew about the gap and allowed for it rather than fixing it: line 892 of `brain-loop.js`, "A ready row is
not counted, as the drain takes none of them: a start for it would end at once and repeat at each tick."

**Was "working" checked live?** No. The orchestrating sessions did the step by hand, so the gap only showed when no
session was steering, overnight.
