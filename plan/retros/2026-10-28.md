## 2. Diagnosis: the tracker refused the deployer's lines for issues with long ids

**What happened.** `fb-loop-assign-step-reads-every-list` (a 36-character id) sat at `needs-owner` from 10-27 09:25 to
02:12 today. The deployer had the owner's approval: `infra.getState` at 02:15 listed `b26963628069…` in `approved`, and
`logs.deploys` has

```
09:25:57  deploy.waiting   brain-x-loop b26963628069
09:26:34  deploy.approved  brain-x-loop b26963628069  by owner
```

The issue's wait named it (`deploy:brain-x-loop@b26963628069`, event 29268). The record has no `receive/` event for
either line. `brain-x-topic`'s own lines for that minute:

```
09:27:46  topic.push.gaveup  topic deploys  name tracker  seq 1437..1440  status 400  tries 1   (four lines)
```

and the core's: four `issue.receive` calls at 09:27:45–46, `400 InvalidRequest`. Entries 1437–1440 of `deploys`
(`topic.read`) are the four `brain-x-loop` lines of this deploy: `dry.run`, `dry.none`, `waiting`, `approved`. Entry
1441, `brain-x-fly`'s approval of the same minute, was taken.

**Why.** `issue.receive` keys its writes `receive/<ray, or id, or entry key>/<kind>/<issue>/comment` (and `/label`).
The deployer's lines have `ray: ""` and a 40-character hex `id`, and the tracker refuses a key over 100 characters
(`_issuesLimits`, `key: 100`). The key is 58 characters and the kind before the issue id and 8 after it, so an id over
34 characters lost an `approved` line, and over 33 a `confirmed` one. The topic does not push a 4xx again, and the subscription `tracker` names no failed topic, so the line was
dropped with one log line no agent reads. A replay with a short key (02:12, event 29608) matched at once:
`{"matched":1,…}`.

What it cost, 10-25 to 10-27, by `measure.py`'s new count (below):

```

fb-library-grant-reaches-owner-notebooks  (40)  1 approval, 1 confirmed, 1 waiting    a security fix held at needs-owner since 10-26 13:58
brain-x-issues-browser-verify-heap        (34)  1 confirmed (10-26 13:45)             at needs-owner since 10-27 07:37
```

`fb-library-grant-reaches-owner-notebooks` is the line-1 case. Its fix (`brain-x-library` `e759fd4a8d1f`: a granted
account passed `setPublic`, `versions` and `index` on any notebook) was `deploy.confirmed` on 10-25 at 13:53:28. The
