## 2. Diagnosis: `deploy.ts` gave the review key to kind `task` only, so a bug or a promoted feedback went to the owner

Chosen because it is the one cause of owner waits this week that is a tool's choice and not the owner's rule (lines 2 and 4). The owner acted in his tab 3 times in the last 24 h, and the issues waiting on him went from 7 to 13; every hold that need not be his adds to that.

**What happened.** `deploy.ts` uses the deployer's review key, after a reviewer's pass of the exact patch, in place of the owner's click. Its condition at `e26458b`, the kind test unchanged since `d1c1137` (11-07, the owner's 16A and 17A; `!served.length` is `1d38a9c`'s, 11-09):

```
// A task only: the owner's words were "task deploy", and deploy-held is known to hold a task's finish, not another kind's.
if (waits.length && reviewKey && issue.state === "open" && issue.kind === "task" && !served.length) {
```

The comments `deploy.ts` wrote over 7 d (`holds.py`, by the reason in the comment's text and the issue's `kind`):

```
("owner's four", task) 37   ("owner, other", task) 36   ("review-first", task) 30
("owner, other", feedback) 17   ("owner, other", bug) 12   ("owner's four", feedback) 10
("path", task) 8   ("path", bug) 5   ("path", feedback) 1
```

"owner, other" is a hold that is neither a patch of the owner's four nor a Worker that serves a path. The 29 on a bug or feedback are 15 issues. Three of them (`approve-link-needs-owner`, `issues-deeplink-not-shown`, `issues-page-phone`, 94.5 h between them) are `brain-x-issues`, which has served `/issues/decide` since `approve-link` (11-08) and would be the owner's anyway; before `1d38a9c` (11-09 08:19) the comment did not name the path. The other 12 waited 49.8 h for his approval in 7 d, and two were open at 02:06: `fb-no-per-worker-cpu` (`brain-x-dashboard`, 18.0 h) and `loop-after-done-reads-wait-note` (`brain-x-loop`, 2.0 h). The second holds the `brain-x-loop` half of `fb-no-wake-on-future-scout` and is the 5th failing test above.

**Why the system allowed it.** The comment gives two reasons. The owner's own words of 16A are not on file in `cloud-brain-dev/`, `plan/` or the tracker's record (searched for `16A`); "task deploy" is the comment's quotation of them, and the commit's title is "review key approves any reviewed task deploy". The same commit quotes 16A elsewhere as "a fresh reviewer's pass of this exact patch stands for the owner's click on any open issue" (`deploy.ts`, the line above `reviewKey`) and "any held service deploy, within the deployer's scope for the review key" (`roles/worker.md:153`); neither names a kind. The second reason holds for a workflow, not a kind. The installed policy (v11, event 28403) maps kinds to workflows:

```
"kinds": {"bug": "task", "task": "task", "feedback": "feedback", "security": "security", "feature": "project", "project": "project"}
```

and the task workflow's finish guard ends `&& !("deploy-held" in issue.labels)`. A bug is on the task workflow from its open. A feedback is promoted to it by `brain-x-loop` (`swapped promote`, e.g. #45523), and a promote keeps the kind (`accept.ts:721`): `issue.get` of `fb-no-per-worker-cpu` gives `kind: feedback`, `workflow: 769f4257…`, which is `issue.policy`'s `names.task`. So `deploy-held` holds the finish of every issue `deploy.ts` runs on but a feedback not yet promoted, and the check excluded two kinds the safety reason covers.

**Would it have been caught?** A worker saw it on 11-09 and left it: "fb-delve-ref-splits-template-link is kind `feedback`, and deploy.ts uses the review key for kind `task` only … The rule is the owner's, so I left it" (#42577). Nothing counted these holds apart from the owner's own: `measure.py` printed approvals by approver, not holds by reason. It does now (section 3).
