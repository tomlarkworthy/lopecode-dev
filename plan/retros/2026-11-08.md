## 5. What others do

The 11-05 retro's reading of the transactional outbox ([backend-engineering-essentials](https://alivedise.github.io/backend-engineering-essentials/distributed-systems/outbox-pattern-and-transactional-messaging)) applies at the source too: the state change and the event are written together, and a relay that retries reads the outbox. The deployer writes `ask/ID` as `approved` in its rows and then sends the line to the topic in a separate call, which may never be made. A relay in the deployer's own tick that appends each approval not yet appended would close it where it starts; the drain's poll closes it at the consumer, 10 to 20 minutes late. No new search by this run.
