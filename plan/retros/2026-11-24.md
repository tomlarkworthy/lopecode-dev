## 2. Diagnosis: a cap that resets at midnight was handed to the owner, and the tracker's work stopped for 5.5 h

At 00:00 UTC the cap counted a new day; nothing read that.

**Why the system allowed it.** `deploy.ts` treats every refusal of the self path alike (`deploy.ts` 516-520 before this run: "A refusal of a gate is quoted in the owner's comment below, and the owner deploys it as before"). The gates differ: the switch off and a sweep hold need the owner, and the day's cap (`brain-deployer.js` 2313-2314, `unattendedDeployerDaily ?? 3`) does not. `roles/worker.md` and the knowledge doc said the same: "A gate that refuses (… the day's cap …) gives `owner-deploy`". The test `a refusal of the review key (a gate of the deployer) is the owner's path as before` covered only the switch. The cap was reached for the first time on 11-23, the first full day with the switch on.

**Is "working" checked against the live system?** The cap was, at its first use: the deployer refused the fourth deploy as designed. What it led to was not checked by anyone: the issues waited on the owner with no comment that the reason had ended.

**Would it have been caught without this run?** The owner's next laptop sitting would have deployed both by hand, a manual act the switch exists to remove. `measure.py` shows the idle buckets but does not name the cause.

**The rule this meets, and where the fix departs from it.** `cloud-brain-dev/AGENTS.md` 57: "Nothing waits on a clock: no timer, sleep-and-retry or calendar hold. A thing that waits for a condition subscribes to the event that changes it […]; where there is no event, add one." A hold to 00:10 UTC is a calendar hold. The way the rule asks for is an event: the deployer logs a line when its cap reopens (its first deploy or tick of a UTC day), and a wait noted for it wakes the issue through `issue.receive`. Its cost, estimated and not built: a change to `brain-deployer.js` and to `issue.receive`'s matching, two patches of which one is the deployer's, so two reviews and a deploy, against 11 lines of `deploy.ts` today. The fix uses the tracker's `issue.hold`, which `route.js` 113 and `brain-x-loop` read ("a held issue starts no machine. Once the hold ends, the label wakes the persona at the next decision"). That decision comes at the loop's cron tick, `*/5 * * * *` (`brain-loop.js` 121), so the issue is taken up to 5 min after 00:10. This is a departure from the rule, for the owner to keep or undo (section 4); undoing it is a revert of `7c2e054`, and the event above is the alternative. The other gates that clear in minutes (a deploy of itself not confirmed, a sweep pending, another change running) were not seen refusing in 7 d and stay the owner's; each has a deployer line a wait could name, which is the event-driven form if they are seen.
