Each worker yield (`issue.ts yield`, keys `K/label`, `K/wait`) replaced a live wait that named the deploy with one that does not: `deployed:…` is not the word `deploy:…`. The put-backs came 8 and 7 minutes later. The same happened on 11-12: `ai-images-review-notes`'s yield #45443 ("deployer's confirmed or put-back of brain-x-ai@cd81c0bf6406 …") replaced #45440 (`deploy:brain-x-ai@cd81c0bf6406`). Three cases in 7 d, all after `deploy.ts` did not end in its own yield: a relay timeout (11-12), "InternalServerError: Network connection lost" (11-14 18:02, #46839, `not-verified` with no `probation` row, so no yield), and a `probation` answer after the review key's approval had put `needs-worker` on (21:09:37, #47275), which `deploy.ts`'s `yieldOnProbation` reads as the deployer's line having come (`deploy.ts:566`), so it took `worker-processing` off and left `needs-worker` on.
