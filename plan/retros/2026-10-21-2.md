## 2. Diagnosis: a test run with no verdict puts a good deploy back, and the browser's reset is the usual cause

```

13:05:56 deploy.put-back    brain-x-pds 7e7f45153b34  not confirmed in 10 minutes
18:33:59 deploy.confirmed   brain-x-pds 7e7f45153b34
```
The same hash ran at 18:33, its 126 tests passing. `ship.ts` commented at 12:55 "the deployer did not verify it … Nothing
was moved" (event 19312), `waiting-deploys.ts --stalled` named it at 15:05 (event 19314), and it waited 5.5 h for a drain
to start and ship it again.

**Since when, and how often.** 7 days of the log: 5 of the 7 put-backs were `not confirmed in 10 minutes`, and 4 of those
5 hashes were deployed again unchanged and confirmed (`brain-x-fly` `59305e98087b` and `brain-x-loop` `0121173b9313`
10-17, `brain-x-dashboard` `bba8289ae0e9` 10-18, `brain-x-pds` `7e7f45153b34` 10-21). Before those four the log has a
run with no verdict: a reset for three, a 402 of `browser.extend` for `brain-x-fly`. The fifth, `brain-x-issues`
`f1b2db622e7f` (2026-10-20 03:07), has `deploy.verify` and then the put-back, with no line between. The other two put-backs were
failed tests. 17 runs gave no verdict (13 dry, 4 verify; 10-20: 7, 10-21: 5), 13 of them with `ERR_CONNECTION_RESET`, 3 "the modules of this Brain could not be read: Failed to fetch", 1 a 402 of `browser.extend`.

**Why the system allowed it.** Three texts each do what they say:

- The deployer reads a run with no verdict as "nothing" (its reference, "no verdict … nothing"), leaves the deploy on
  probation, and its tick puts back a probation of 10 minutes.
- `rollback.ts` read every `restores` row of `logs.deploys` as final, so after the re-deploy of 18:33 its sweep wrote
  at 18:34 "rolled-back: brain-x-pds 7e7f45153b34 is rolled back … goes to not-done" and the label `rolled-back` on
  `pds-server-auth` (events 19316, 19317). No move event was written and the issue stayed `done`; why the move did
  not land was not read (the sweep says a refused write on stderr only). All 4
  `rolled-back:` comments of 7 days are this case: `fly-no-day-hours-limit` and `loops-decision-and-tokens-per-loop`
  (10-18 09:41), `ad-9-panel-layout` (10-18 20:55), `pds-server-auth`.

**Was "working" checked live?** The browser's tests use a fake whose sites fail always or never. The sweep's test feeds

## 3. Changes made by this run

1. **`brain-x-browser`: a load that resets is tried again in the same tab.** `navigate` retries
   `net::ERR_CONNECTION_RESET` and `_CLOSED` with pauses of 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 4 … s until the load's time is up (half of a
   run's `timeout`, 30 s at most; the deployer's runs have 100 s, so 30 s). Every other error fails at once as before. A
   final failure says how many tries it made. The fake site takes `failFirst`; the test
   `test_browser_run_tries_a_reset_connection_again` (2 resets then a load; a reset to the end; `ERR_NAME_NOT_RESOLVED`
   at once). The reference row of `browser.run` says it. `bun test.ts brain-x-browser`: 19 pass, 0 fail, 1 needs a
   browser.
   - **Deployed** under the deploy lock (taken 18:52:02, released 18:56:29) with `apply.ts brain-x-browser
     --reason="retro 2026-10-21-2: …"`: hash `1a50544deaee`, version `ea447c2a`, `verified`, 20 tests (the deployer's
     browser run passed the in-page test too). Its four dry runs just before had no verdict, each `ERR_CONNECTION_RESET`.
   - **Checked live, the retry itself not seen:** 4 new browsers of the owner, 18:53 to 18:56, each loaded the Brain's `verify.html` at its first
     `browser.run` (1.3 to 2.6 s). Before the deploy, 2 of 3 new browsers and 4 of 4 deployer runs reset at first. Four
     trials is not a rate; whether a retry happened inside them is not logged. The next deploys' `deploy.dry.none` count
     is the measure.
   - Limits. A `browser.run` with no `timeout` gives its load 15 s, under the 20 s reset measured on `retro2`; the
     deployer's runs give 30 s. `open` and `goto` now wait up to 30 s on a host that really resets, where they failed
