## 2. Diagnosis: the database slowed from 10:00, and a held deploy stopped reaching the owner

**What was seen first.** `i12589-blob-deployer`'s worker dry-ran a `brain-x-blob` patch at 17:06; the deployer held it
and gave a link valid to 18:06 (event 19833). `deploy.ts` yielded with `waiting-deploy`. By the redesign (plan §3.4) the
deployer's `deploy.waiting` line goes to the topic `deploys`, whose push to `issue.receive` swaps `waiting-deploy` for
`needs-owner`, which puts the issue in the owner's attention list. The record has the comment (19839, 17:07:59) and no
label. The core's log for that call:

```

17:08:01.964  issue.receive  holder brain-x-topic   status 400            ms 9233
17:08:02.546  topic.push.gaveup  topic deploys  name tracker  status 400  tries 2
```

The deployer sends no DM of a hold (its reference, "`request` is `{ id, url, code, until }`", and no `bluesky` in
`brain-deployer.js`). So the owner was shown nothing, and the link ended at 18:06. No other event was written
between 19839 and the failure, so no other writer took the seq; the database refused the insert.

**Why a database error ended the push.** `brain-x-issues`' `run` answers a thrown error with its `status`, and the
core's `db.sql` error carries 400 (`cloudflare-iac.js`, `core()`; `brain-db.js:273`, `fail(c, 400, "SqlError", …)`).
`brain-x-topic` tries a 5xx again five times over 15 minutes and gives a 4xx up at once (its reference, "the target
answers"). A failure of the tracker's own database therefore reached the topic as the caller's fault.

**Reproduced.** The same line sent to `issue.receive` with the owner's session at 19:13 answered 200 in 3.9 s and wrote
label 19866 (`needs-owner` on, `waiting-deploy` off). The failure was not in the line.
