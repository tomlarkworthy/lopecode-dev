**Two defects.**

1. `deploy.ts` cuts its wait note at 200 characters (`deploy.ts:210`, `note.slice(0, 200)`, as `issue.wait` refuses a longer note). Six `brain-x-*` words of 30 to 37 characters came to 208 with their spaces, so the sixth became `deploy:brain-x-container@fc83`. `issue.receive` matches a whole word of a 12-hex hash, so the container's lines reached no issue. With 12 hex digits each, the six words of `ml-migrate-pins-2`, `-3` and `-4` come to 201, 205 and 210 characters, so each would lose its sixth Worker the same way. `ml-migrate-pins-2` began at 02:19 on the machine's older checkout, before this run's change reached it.
2. `issue.ts yield --label waiting-deploy` takes `needs-worker` off when it is on (`issue.ts`, "After deploy.ts answered `arrived: true` …"), and it does not ask whether a line is still to come. The 11-15 change made it keep the deploy words of the live waits. All five whole words had their confirmed lines on the issue already, so the wait could end only at its `until`, 24 h on. The worker's own note names `deployed:brain-x-container@fc…`, the one Worker with no line on the issue. Its record was not read for this report.

**Why the system allowed it.** The worker brief says that after `arrived: true` a worker goes on to the record and the hand (`roles/worker.md`, `sent` with `arrived: true`). It does not say what to do when one Worker's line is missing, and the tool let the worker wait on lines that had come. The watchdog repairs `waiting-*` only once the wait has lapsed (`brain-loop.js`, "The watchdog"), so the floor was 24 h.

**Was "working" checked against the live system?** By `measure.py`, yes: its section "open issues at waiting-deploy whose wait names a hash with no deployer line since that ends it" printed `ml-migrate-pins-1` today. Nothing acts on that section between retros. The 11-15 check, "waiting-deploy with no live deploy wait", printed 0, because the wait was live.

**Would it have been caught without this run?** Yes, at 09:27 today, when the wait lapsed and the watchdog put `needs-worker` back. That is 24 h from the yield and 7 h more than it had already cost.
