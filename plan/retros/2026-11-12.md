So the owner's approval of a live change also gates every issue after it. Two ways out, both changing a rule that is his:
- (a) an `after` link to a `major` issue is met once its post-deploy review has passed with a `deployed:` ref; the finish stays his. Cost: if he sends one back, which has not happened in 43, the dependents were built on a change that then changes.
- (b) drain2 finishes a `major` issue whose post-deploy review passed, and tells him. Cost: removes an approval that `philosophy.md` names as his.

My pick is (a); it keeps his approval and takes it off the path of other work. Not done: it changes what his approval holds, which is his to decide (section 4).
