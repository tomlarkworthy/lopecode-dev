## 2a. Diagnosis: the quiet tick was checked on a rig that had none of the live tracker's waiting issues

**What happened.** `idle-tracker-reads` asked `brain-x-loop` to stop deciding whole every 5 minutes when nothing changed.
The worker added "a quiet tick": a tick decision that changed nothing keeps a row `quiet`, and the next tick repeats it
with no tracker read (`398e3efd2b10`, 11-01 06:15; `8273ac7934e3`, confirmed 07:43, for `delve-scout-loop-rule`, which changed
`loopRoute` and not the watchdog or `still`, ran all night). The review passed it on the module's rig: `test_loop_an_idle_hour_…` counted 1 decision an hour.
On cb4 in the 4 idle hours after (11-01 22:00Z to 11-02 02:00Z):

```
brain-x-loop lines     loop.decide 48   loop.quiet 0
core, issue.* by       brain-x-loop issue.list 768, issue.get 480, issue.policy 48;
                       issue.get 192 under delegation f5d105c078aa (the watchdog's)
loop.status, last      "watchdog":{"wrote":[],"read":4,"left":0}
```

`memo.calm` (`brain-loop.js`, the line after `const still`) required the watchdog's `read` to be 0. The watchdog
reads the events of a `waiting-issue` issue with no live wait, and leaves it when its newest label is the route Before
after's. Four such issues were open all night (`issues-store-object`, `-relocate`, `-cutover`, `-drop-d1-tables`, the
tracker's move off D1). 4 reads a tick × 12 ticks = the 48 an hour of `issue.get` under the delegation, and no tick was
idle. What such a read judges changes only with an event, which already ends a quiet run.

**Why the system allowed it.** The rig's idle hour had a waiting-deploy issue with a live wait, a `needs-owner` issue and
a held one: none that the watchdog reads. The reviewer ran the test with behaviours taken out and checked that the live
module was the patched one, but read nothing live after the deploy. `loop.status`, which an owner's token can read,
showed `read: 4` all night. The issue's acceptance named this read as the retro's, so it was caught at the first retro
after the deploy, 20 h later.

**Alternatives for next time.** (a) A worker's acceptance on an "idle" or "fewer calls" claim reads `loop.status` or
the deployer's own count after one tick, which a token can; (b) the rig's idle hour is seeded from `issue.list` as cb4
answers it; (c) the retro reads it, as today. (c) held this time at a cost of one day; no rule was added.
