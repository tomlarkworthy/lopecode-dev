## 3. Why the loop could go 15 hours without a decision

Two of the last three nights the loop received no event for hours: 5 h after the cutover (11-03) and 15 h after 11-04's deploy. Each time the cause was in `brain-x-issues`, and each time `brain-x-loop`, the consumer, had no way to notice. Since the 11-02 retro a quiet tick makes no tracker call at all. The outbox write-ups cited above keep a poll of the record behind the push for this reason. Today's change bounds a quiet run at `quietMaxMinutes` (60): one whole decision an hour while idle. Estimated from the rig, not measured on cb4: a whole idle decision reads the tracker about 15 to 20 times (the loop's own test of an idle tick counts 15 `issue.list`), so this costs about 20 tracker calls an idle hour, where a quiet hour made none. The tracker is now an object, so these calls go to its Durable Object, not to D1; they add to the object's duration that `issues-store-cutover`'s 7-day read (R10) measures. Not done: a cursor of the last tracker seq told against the tracker's head, which would log the stop itself rather than bound it.
