Scanning the same frame down its columns inverts that: measured y, extrapolated x. The column pass is the same detector applied to a quarter-turned frame, so it shares every threshold and gate with the row pass. Both passes cost two scans. They run concurrently across the worker pool, which is why this is on by default: on the phone the frame goes from about 20.5ms to about 26ms against a 33.3ms budget at the 30fps camera cap, rather than halving the frame rate as a sequential second pass did.
